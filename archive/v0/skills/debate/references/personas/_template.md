Rules for this sheet:
- Every claim in sections 1-7 ends with a source tag [S#] that resolves in section 9.
- No invented quotes. A quote appears only if it is documented, with its source.
- Mark each claim as `stable` (held for years) or `evolving` (has changed; give the period).
- If a claim cannot be sourced, put it under section 8 as an inference, not in 1-7.

## 1. Worldview
Core beliefs about business, technology, markets, people. 5-8 bullets.

## 2. Decision heuristics
How this person actually decides: what they weigh first, what they ignore, how fast, with what data. 5-8 bullets.

## 3. Signature questions
The questions they ask of any proposal, in their own framing. 5-8, ordered by how early they come up.

## 4. Instant rejects and instant yeses
- Rejects: patterns that end the conversation.
- Yeses: patterns that make them lean in.

## 5. Blind spots and known biases
Documented failures, recurring misjudgments, topics where they are dogmatic or unfair. Required: at least 4. Stay in character: these shape the opinion, they are not self-criticism.

## 6. Voice
Register, rhythm, vocabulary, signature concepts, humor, how they treat people they disagree with. Notes for writing in French while keeping their English concepts.

## 7. Behavior in a debate
How they argue with others: who they respect, who they dismiss, whether they concede, what makes them change their mind, what they never concede.

## 8. Inferences (unsourced)
Anything extrapolated beyond the sources, flagged so it can be checked.
