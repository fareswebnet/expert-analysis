# Report template

Write in French. Fill every section. Quick mode: sections 1, 3, 5 and 6 only, each short; keep the transcript in the annex.

```markdown
# Board : <subject>

- Date : <YYYY-MM-DD>
- Mode : complet | éclair
- Jury : <names, with the reason for each bench member>
- Dossier validé par l'utilisateur : oui

## 1. Positions

| Expert | Position | Objection principale | Ce qui le ferait changer d'avis |
|---|---|---|---|

Positions are Go, Go sous conditions, Pivot (change the plan), Pas encore (the direction may be right, the proof is missing), Non. No numeric score. Show round 1 and final position when they differ, and the proof each expert requires.

## 2. Carte des accords et des désaccords

- **Accords** : points of fact or method on which every expert converges. Mark each with its proof level (fait, inférence, hypothèse, spéculation) and say when an expert drew market evidence from a figure the dossier gives as an assumption.
- **Hypothèses partagées non vérifiées** : assumptions the experts all held without checking them (who pays, what the product really is, which segment, which price). Never list these as agreements. Say whether round 4 contested them.
- **Zone d'accord et dissidences** : what every expert accepts, stated as conditions, then the dissent that remains expert by expert. Round 4 (refutation) results go here, with any new objection it produced.
- **Désaccords réels** : each divergence, who stands where, and why. If a duel took place: the point, the two experts, the turns in two lines each, the jurors' verdicts.
- **Axes de tension** : the axes named before round 2, who stood where, and whether the round 2 and 3 answers moved anyone.
- **Hypothèses qui expliquent les désaccords** : for each real disagreement, the assumption that, if true, makes one side right.
- **Questions sans réponse dans le dossier** : table `Question | Posée par | Document ou donnée qui y répondrait | Effet sur le verdict si la réponse est oui / non`, ordered by effect on the verdict, not by ease of getting the answer. Collect the questions the experts asked and the dossier could not answer; do not invent any.
- **Risques que personne n'a soulevés** : yours, labeled as the facilitator's.

## 3. Recommandation du facilitateur (synthèse : la décision reste à l'utilisateur)

Go | Go sous conditions | Pivot | Pas encore | Non, in two or three sentences. If the experts do not agree, say so and name what separates them. Do not present a consensus that did not happen. It is an argued decision, not an average of opinions.

**Conditions de pertinence** : for each option the board considered (go as is, go with conditions, pivot, stop), the conditions under which it is the right one.

## 4. Limites

Where the experts spoke outside what their sheets document, and which dossier hypotheses carry the verdict.

## 5. Tests et critères d'arrêt

| Priorité | Condition ou risque | Hypothèse testée | Test à mener | Mesure | Seuil de réussite (fixé avant le test) | Échantillon et durée | Critère d'arrêt | Ce que le résultat change au verdict | Action associée |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Each row is an experiment: the threshold is written before the test is run, so that the result cannot be reinterpreted afterwards. Order the rows by what the result changes in the verdict, not by how easy the test is. Each row traces to an expert's objection or a risk from section 2. Every expert objection kept in section 1 has at least one row, or a stated reason why it cannot be tested. Do not give an expected gain unless a source or an expert's reasoning supports it; never invent one.

## 6. Dossier

The validated dossier, verbatim.

## Annexe : transcript

The round 1 brief exactly as sent (once, since it is identical for every expert), then round 1, round 2, round 3 (full mode), duel and jury (if any), recadrages with the reason. Every answer verbatim, labeled by expert and round. A failed answer is kept and marked.
```
